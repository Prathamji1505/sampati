# Sampati event pitch

## One-line overview

Sampati connects reviewed flood-impact evidence to human-confirmed relief needs, with traceable source information and decisions.

## Two-minute spoken script

Imagine coordinating relief after a flood. An assessment describes an affected area. A local team reports a need for supplies. Resource updates arrive separately. Before organising help, you need to understand what has been checked, where the information came from, and what still needs confirmation.

I am Pratham, and I am building Sampati: a civilian disaster-response and relief-coordination project.

The working local prototype lets a coordinator preview and record a dated flood assessment, inspect its evidence and uncertainty, and record a reasoned review. From a reviewed incident, a person can create a linked relief request and enter the locally confirmed supply quantity and unit. Corrections and status changes keep an attributed history.

Sampati also retains its aircraft-report foundation: reviewed CSV and Excel imports, source freshness, conflict checks and organisation workspaces. These reports support coordination; operators retain dispatch authority.

The next modules are aid inventory and delivery tracking. A live SATQueryAI connector is also planned around the implemented incident contract. The long-term response chain is impact evidence, reviewed needs, verified resources, human-approved response and confirmed delivery.

The prototype uses fictional data. We have not deployed it in a disaster or measured welfare outcomes. The next validation step is to test it with civilian coordinators and measure review accuracy, task completion and follow-up of unmet needs.

We are looking for civilian workflow feedback, mentors and pilot partners to help build that next step.

## Demo sequence

1. Open Incident review and preview a fictional assessment before recording it.
2. Inspect the dates, evidence and uncertainty; review it with a reason.
3. Open a linked relief draft and enter the requesting organisation, supplies, quantity and unit.
4. Submit/review the request and inspect its attributed history.
5. Show supporting aircraft reports, then present inventory and delivery tracking as the next milestones.
